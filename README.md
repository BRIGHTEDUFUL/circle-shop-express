# Circle Shop Express

this is the logo for the store. Build everything in one go according to the attached app brief, design specifications, and store logo.

Correction applied: remove Pay in Store: At the Circle shop counter. Only two payment methods:
1. Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) with transaction reference submission for manual confirmation.
2. Cash on Delivery (COD) on courier arrival.
In-store pickup at the Circle shop in Accra remains available as a fulfillment collection option.

Implement the full store:
- Design tokens & typography (Archivo / Public Sans / Mono), custom liquid/frosted glass layers over real photography, high contrast, mobile-first responsive layout.
- Global floating header, mega menu, search overlay with instant suggestions, and mobile bottom navigation.
- Home page with interactive workspace setup hotspots, floating glass product card, 4-point Circle/Accra trust strip, category tiles, and product rails.
- Full catalogue across 5 categories (Gaming & Office Desks, Office Chairs, Computer Accessories, Stands & Mounts, Streaming & Audio) with GH₵ currency, specs, and stock indicators.
- Product details with image gallery, specs table, local delivery calculator, and quick WhatsApp enquiry.
- Cart drawer with live totals and free-delivery progress bar.
- Streamlined 3-step checkout with delivery zones (Accra Central, Greater Accra, Nationwide) vs. Circle in-store pickup, Mobile Money instructions & reference entry, and Cash on Delivery.
- Order confirmation with receipt printing and reference lookup tracker.
- Staff operations dashboard to verify submitted MoMo references and update order status.
